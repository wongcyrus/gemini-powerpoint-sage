# Visual Assets Guide for Blog Post

This guide provides detailed instructions for creating the visual assets referenced in the blog post `blog_post.md`.

## Blog Post Banner (Medium Header)

**Goal:** A captivating, high-quality header image that instantly communicates the themes of AI, global education, and transformation. It needs to look great on Medium (recommended size: 2400x1350px or similar 16:9 ratio).

**Visual Concept:** "The Global Classroom of the Future"

**Key Elements:**
1.  **Central Focus:** A stylized, glowing representation of a presentation slide or a holographic screen floating in the center.
2.  **AI & Tech Layer:** Surrounding the screen are subtle, interconnected nodes or digital streams (representing the 10 agents) flowing into it. The color palette should use Google's Gemini-inspired colors (deep blues, purples, and sparks of white/cyan).
3.  **Human/Education Layer:** On the periphery, silhouettes of diverse students or abstract representations of learning (like open books transforming into digital birds) to symbolize accessibility and freedom of knowledge.
4.  **Global Reach:** A subtle background map of the world or connecting lines spanning across a globe, indicating the multilingual/global aspect.

**Style:**
*   **Aesthetic:** Modern, clean, slightly futuristic but warm and inviting (not cold/dystopian).
*   **Colors:** Deep Indigo/Navy background (#1A237E) with vibrant accents in Cyan (#00E5FF), Electric Purple (#AA00FF), and Soft White.
*   **Text (Optional):** If text is included, it should be minimal, e.g., "Gemini PowerPoint Sage" in a clean, modern sans-serif font. However, image-only is often better for Medium headers.

**Prompt Idea for Image Gen:**
> "A futuristic, wide cinematic illustration for a tech blog header. In the center, a glowing holographic presentation screen showing 'Education for All'. Around it, streams of digital data in blue and purple flow like magic. Silhouettes of diverse students from around the world are learning from it. A subtle background of a digital globe. High quality, detailed, vibrant colors, 8k resolution, modern vector art style mixed with 3D render."

## Figure 1: Architecture Diagram

**Goal:** Illustrate the Supervisor-led Multi-Agent workflow in a clean, easy-to-understand diagram, emphasizing how it supports content transformation for diverse educational needs.

**Visual Elements:**
1.  **Input:** A document icon labeled "PowerPoint Slide" (perhaps with a globe or a student silhouette subtly in the background).
2.  **Central Node:** A robot/brain icon labeled "**Supervisor Agent**".
3.  **Satellite Nodes (connected to Supervisor):**
    *   **Overviewer:** Icon representing a "big picture" or map (global context for education).
    *   **Auditor:** Icon with a checklist or magnifying glass (ensuring quality and accessibility standards).
    *   **Analyst:** Icon with an eye or camera (scanning slide visuals for descriptive details).
    *   **Writer:** Icon with a pen or quill (crafting engaging, accessible speaker notes).
    *   **Designer:** Icon with a paintbrush or palette (creating enhanced, readable visuals).
    *   **Translator:** Icon with multiple language symbols (for multilingual content).
    *   **Prompt Rewriter:** Icon showing a gear or a transformational arrow (for style integration).
    *   **Video Generator:** Film reel icon (for immersive content).
4.  **Flow:**
    *   Arrow from **Input** to **Overviewer** (Step 1).
    *   Arrow from **Overviewer** to **Supervisor** (Context).
    *   Circular arrows between **Supervisor** and the satellite agents (Analyst, Writer, etc.) to show coordination.
    *   Arrow from from **Supervisor** to **Output** (Final, Accessible Presentation with Notes and Visuals).

**Style:** Clean, modern, technical but accessible. Use Google colors (Blue, Red, Yellow, Green) if possible to align with the brand. Ensure the visual flow highlights collaboration towards an accessible outcome.

## Figure 2: Style Comparison (Side-by-Side)

**Goal:** Show the dramatic difference the "Style System" makes on both visuals and text, illustrating how engaging themes can transform educational content.

**Layout:** Split screen. Left side = "Corporate/Professional Style", Right side = "Cyberpunk Style".

**Left Side: Professional/Corporate Style**
*   **Visual:** A clean, corporate slide. White background, navy blue accents. A neat bar chart showing "Q4 Learning Metrics". Clean sans-serif font (Arial or Roboto), clear, concise layout.
*   **Speaker Notes Overlay (Callout):** "Analyzing our Q4 learning engagement data, we observe a 15% increase in student participation, primarily driven by our interactive module adoption."
*   **Vibe:** Authoritative, clear, business-like educational content.

**Right Side: Cyberpunk Style**
*   **Visual:** Dark background, neon pink and electric blue grids. The same chart is now a glowing holographic projection. Glitch effects on the text. Font is futuristic/terminal style. A student in a futuristic setting looking at a holographic display.
*   **Speaker Notes Overlay (Callout):** "Jack into the data stream, edgerunners! Our Q4 learning metrics are spiking—a 15% gain in engagement. Learners are jacking into our interactive modules like there's no tomorrow."
*   **Vibe:** Edgy, high-tech, rebellious, making learning feel like an adventure in a dystopian future.

## Figure 3: Veo Video Background

**Goal:** Demonstrate a motion background generated by Veo, suitable for an engaging educational title slide.

**Visual Elements:**
*   **Content:** A looping abstract background that feels dynamic and futuristic, or a subtle animation related to the presentation's topic.
*   **Description:** "A seamless, looping animation of digital data streams flowing across a dark background, with subtle glowing blue and green particles. The movement is fluid and mesmerizing, suitable for an opening educational slide."
*   **Overlay:** Text on top saying "The Future of Learning" or "AI in Education" (to show it's a slide background).
*   **Format:** GIF or MP4 (looping).

**Note for Creation:** If you cannot generate a real Veo video, a high-quality stock footage GIF of abstract digital data, or flowing energy patterns will suffice to demonstrate the concept.

## Figure 4: Inclusive Education Impact Infographic

**Goal:** Visually summarize the benefits of Gemini PowerPoint Sage for accessibility and inclusive education, directly aligning with the "Real-World Impact" section of the blog post.

**Layout:** An infographic or a multi-panel visual with a clean, inspiring design.

**Elements (each in a distinct, well-designed panel or section):**
1.  **Visually Impaired Learners:**
    *   **Icon:** An ear with sound waves, or a pair of headphones.
    *   **Text:** "Rich Audio Descriptions: Visuals explained in detail for auditory learners."
2.  **Multilingual Students:**
    *   **Icon:** A globe with multiple speech bubbles in different languages (e.g., English, Chinese, Spanish).
    *   **Text:** "16+ Languages & Cultural Adaptation: Content translated and localized for native understanding."
3.  **Engaging Content:**
    *   **Icon:** A lightbulb with a spark, or a smiling student with a thought bubble.
    *   **Text:** "Engaging & Themed Learning: Dry facts transformed into captivating narratives."
4.  **Educator Efficiency:**
    *   **Icon:** A teacher figure looking relaxed or smiling, perhaps with a clock showing saved time.
    *   **Text:** "Educator Empowerment: Focus on teaching, not manual adaptation."

**Overall Vibe:** Inspiring, clear, and action-oriented. Use a clean, modern aesthetic with good use of white space. Colors should be vibrant but professional, perhaps echoing Google's brand palette. This visual should convey the core mission of making education accessible and enjoyable for everyone.
